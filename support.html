FAQ

How can I get an APK File?

The mobile apps that we based the projects on are no longer available on the Google Play Store. Sonic Retro maintains archived copies on their wiki. Sonic 1 can be found here and Sonic 2 can be found here

How do I install and patch S1F & S2A?

Our installer now handles everything. Launch it, choose your installation folder, and add the APK or RSDK file. If the game is already in the target folder, it will be updated but your progress and settings will be kept.

Will S1F & S2A work with a data.RSDK file?

Yes. Supplying a data.rsdk file will skip the automated APK unpacking part of the install process. Please ensure that the filename ends in .rsdk

Are the games compatible with operating systems other than Windows?

Our installer is programmed for Windows, but should run fine on Wine or Proton. Once this is done, a Windows executable, a Linux appimage, and an Android APK will be available in the game folder. Android compaitibility is still in progress, and you may encounter unexpected issues. A native Linux installer is currently being worked on.

Where can I report bugs or seek technical help?

You can talk to us on Bluesky (teamforever.bsky.social) or send us an email at teamforeverhelp@gmail.com

Where can I find or submit mods?

Gamebanana hosts the vast majority of mods made by our community. You can also find this community at the Retro Engine Modding Server on Discord but be aware this is not a Team Forever-specific server.

How do I use mods?

Make a folder within your game folder and call it 'Mods'. Mods you have downloaded should be able to be unzipped into that folder, and can then be enabled in the game menus.

I have save files from the original decomp that don't show. Where are they?

Our projects look for saved games in a different part of the save file in order to have more room for saved games. This is why your vanilla saves won't appear automatically. If you press the UP button on the Sega screen five times, the game will try to copy thiese saves across. You only need to do this once.

Where did the Team Forever Twitter account go?

Team Forever have chosen to move away from Twitter as a platform. All new updates will be posted on the site. Additional announcements, trailers and sneak peeks can be found at our YouTube page and our Bluesky page.

Are these games compatible with Sonic Origins?

The installers for Sonic Forever and Sonic 2 Absolute will not be able to process the RSDK files from Sonic Origins.The data files in Sonic Origins are packed in a different format, and do not contain music and sound effects files.

Are you going to port these projects to iOS/MacOS?

Apple devices tend to have a much more closed eco-system, so making custom software as a hobbyist is more difficult. Currently we have neither the skills or the desire to port to those devices.
